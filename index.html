SpM-lab

Compact representations and analytic continuation of imaginary-time Green's functions

Open-source libraries for quantum many-body calculations: the intermediate representation (IR), the discrete Lehmann representation (DLR), sparse sampling, minimal pole representations, and sparse-modeling and Nevanlinna analytic continuation.

sparse-ir ecosystem

Libraries for the IR and DLR bases and sparse sampling in Python, Julia, Rust, C and Fortran, sharing one backend and one set of conventions.

MiniPole and ESPRIT
